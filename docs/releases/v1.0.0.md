# A little capture. A big impression.

Meet **Cutter 1.0**, an open-source screen capture and composition studio by **Apkeo**. Capture a piece of your screen, give it room to breathe, and share something beautifully finished — all on your own computer.

![Cutter editor](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/apkeo/cutter/v1.0.0/docs/screenshots/editor.png)

## What's inside

- A screen-region picker you can move and resize from any edge or corner.
- Screenshots and recordings saved automatically before you edit.
- Non-destructive source crop and timeline in/out controls.
- Custom canvas dimensions, aspect presets and one-click fit to media.
- Pixel-perfect corner radius, padding and a hex background picker.
- Composition FPS capped to the source frame rate.
- Import by drag-and-drop; export PNG, JPEG, WebP, MP4, MOV, WebM and GIF.
- Copy the exported file or its path, or reveal it in Explorer/Finder.
- Persistent preferences and last project; English and Polish interfaces.
- No account, subscription, cloud upload or telemetry.

**Default shortcut:** `Ctrl+Shift+2` on Windows; `Cmd+Shift+2` on macOS. The same shortcut stops recording. Change it in Settings.

## First launch

These community builds are **not signed with a commercial code-signing certificate or notarized by Apple**. Windows SmartScreen or macOS Gatekeeper may ask you to confirm opening the application. On macOS, use **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** if required; you do not need to disable Gatekeeper globally.

macOS also requires **Screen & System Audio Recording** permission for Cutter. Grant it in Privacy & Security and restart the app. Recordings capture the screen image only; audio from imported videos is retained in MP4, MOV and WebM exports. GIF has no audio.

macOS packages are built and import/export-tested on native Apple Silicon and Intel runners. Screen capture uses Electron's platform capture API; interactive macOS screen-permission approval cannot be exercised by the hosted release runner.
